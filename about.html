Our Philosophy

A scent is the most personal thing you wear.

It arrives before you do and lingers after you leave. It marks occasions, recalls places and quietly becomes part of how people remember you.

That's why we believe choosing a fragrance deserves more than a quick scroll through an endless list. NeoFragrances exists to make that choice feel considered — a focused collection, clear information and people you can talk to.

What We Stand For

Three things guide everything we do

I

Curation over clutter

A collection shaped around houses people genuinely love — from designer classics to the richness of Arabian perfumery — rather than everything at once.

II

Clarity at every step

Prices shown in Ghana cedis before you check out, stock shown on every product, and order status you can follow yourself.

III

Real people, real help

When you have a question, you reach us directly — by phone, email or WhatsApp — not a ticket queue.
