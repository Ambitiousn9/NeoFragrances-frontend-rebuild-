Common Questions

Quick answers

How do I track my order?

Use Track an Order with your order number and the email you ordered with. If you're signed in, all your orders are listed under My Orders.

I didn't receive my verification or password-reset email.

Check your spam or junk folder first. You can request a new verification link from the login page, or a new reset link from Forgot Password. Still nothing? Contact us and we'll help.

How do I pay for my order?

Checkout is completed securely through Paystack. Once your payment is confirmed, your order appears under My Orders and a confirmation is sent to your email.

Can you help me choose a fragrance?

Of course. Send us a message on WhatsApp with the scents you usually enjoy, and we'll suggest options from the collection.

Where can I find your shipping and returns information?

Our shipping and returns pages are being prepared. Until they're published, please contact us before ordering if you have any questions about delivery or returns.
